## Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how **Waive Ltd** (company number 16878413) and **myWaive Ltd** (company number 17385164), both registered at 3 Lloyd's Avenue, London, England, EC3N 3DS (together, "Waive", "we", "us"), collect and use personal data.

Waive Ltd is the data controller for this website (www.mywaive.io). Where the myWaive platform is provided to you through your pension provider, employer or benefits provider, that organisation is usually the data controller and Waive acts as a data processor under a written contract with them. Their privacy notice will tell you more about how they use your data.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at [privacy@mywaive.io](mailto:privacy@mywaive.io) or write to the address above.

## The data we collect

**When you use this website**, we collect:

- Information you give us when you request a demo or contact us: your name, work email address, organisation, role, approximate organisation size and anything you write in your message.
- Technical information collected automatically: IP address, browser type and version, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit. See our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy) for how we use cookies.

**When you use the myWaive platform** through your provider or employer, the platform processes the information needed to give you personalised financial guidance and advice. Depending on how your organisation has configured myWaive, this may include your name, date of birth, employment and salary details, pension scheme membership and contribution history, fund holdings, other pensions and savings you tell us about, your goals and attitude to risk, and the recommendations you receive and the decisions you take.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

## How we use your data and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Responding to demo requests and enquiries | Legitimate interests (running and promoting our business) |
| Sending you information you have asked for about myWaive | Legitimate interests; consent where required by law |
| Operating, securing and improving this website | Legitimate interests |
| Providing the myWaive platform to your provider or employer | Performance of our contract with them (we act as processor) |
| Creating and retaining an audit trail of recommendations made through the platform | Legal obligation and legitimate interests (regulatory record-keeping, including FCA COBS 9 requirements of our regulated partners) |
| Complying with law, regulation and lawful requests | Legal obligation |

Where we process special category data (for example, health information that affects your retirement planning), we do so only with your explicit consent or as otherwise permitted by law, and only where your organisation has enabled that functionality.

## Automated decision-making

myWaive uses automated systems, including AI-enabled components, to generate personalised financial guidance and advice. Every recommendation is produced within defined, tested parameters, is validated against applicable regulatory rules and is logged. You always decide whether to act on a recommendation. If you are a platform user you can ask for a human review of any recommendation by contacting your provider or employer, or us at the address above.

## Who we share data with

We share personal data only where necessary:

- **Your pension provider, employer or benefits provider**, where they are the controller and myWaive is delivered on their behalf.
- **Regulated partners** who hold the relevant regulatory permissions for advice delivered through the platform.
- **Service providers** who host and support our systems, including Microsoft (Azure hosting, UK regions) and our form and email providers, under contracts that require them to protect your data.
- **Professional advisers, regulators and law enforcement** where required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell personal data.

## International transfers

Our platform is hosted on Microsoft Azure with primary data residency in the United Kingdom and geo-redundant backups in a second UK region. Where a service provider processes data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement (or Addendum) and appropriate safeguards.

## How long we keep data

- Demo requests and enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
- Website analytics: see our Cookie Policy.
- Platform data and audit trails: for the period required by our contract with your organisation and by applicable regulatory record-keeping rules, which for advice records is typically at least 5 years (and indefinitely for pension transfer advice).

## Security

We use encrypted storage, encryption in transit (TLS), private networking, role-based access control and threat monitoring. Your organisation's data is held in a dedicated, logically isolated environment. We are implementing an ISO 27001-aligned control programme.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You also have rights in relation to automated decision-making.

To exercise your rights, contact [privacy@mywaive.io](mailto:privacy@mywaive.io). If we act as processor for your organisation, we may direct your request to them. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.
