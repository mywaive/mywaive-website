## Introduction

Waive Ltd and myWaive Ltd (together "Waive") are committed to preventing modern slavery and human trafficking in our business and supply chain. Waive is currently below the turnover threshold at which the Modern Slavery Act 2015 requires a statement. We publish this statement voluntarily because we believe our customers, partners and the members and employees who use myWaive should expect it of us.

## Our business

Waive is a UK financial technology company. We build and operate myWaive, a cloud-hosted platform that delivers personalised, regulated financial advice to pension scheme members and employees through pension providers, employers and benefits providers. We are headquartered in London and our team is based in the United Kingdom.

## Our supply chain

Our supply chain is short and consists mainly of technology and professional services: cloud hosting (Microsoft Azure, UK regions), software tools, data providers, and legal, compliance, accounting and recruitment services. We do not manufacture goods and do not source from sectors generally considered high risk for forced labour.

## Our policies and approach

- We pay all employees at least the Real Living Wage and comply with UK employment law, including right-to-work checks.
- We expect suppliers to comply with the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and equivalent laws, and we include this expectation in our standard supplier terms.
- We assess new suppliers for modern slavery risk as part of onboarding, with greater scrutiny for any supplier outside the UK or in a higher-risk sector.
- Staff can raise concerns about any unethical practice directly with the CEO or through our whistleblowing process, without fear of reprisal.

## Effectiveness and next steps

As an early-stage company we review this statement annually. Over the next 12 months we will formalise supplier due-diligence questions, provide awareness training to all staff, and review this statement against the Home Office guidance as the business grows.

## Approval

This statement was approved by the board of Waive Ltd and is signed on its behalf by James Brincat, Founder & CEO.
